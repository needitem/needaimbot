FPS aim · view-rotation model

Rotating the view to bring the target to the crosshair

Drag on the view to move the target; let go and it holds still — watch the controller rotate the camera to centre the crosshair on it. Data lands ~1 frame late (144Hz), so the filter/controller converge against that delay. The crosshair is fixed at centre; the mouse turns the view through a perspective lens, so a fixed mouse count moves the target more near the edges than the centre.

Why the lens matters. Screen offset = focal · tan(angle), so a mouse count that turns the view a fixed number of degrees shoves a target near the edge much further than one near the centre. The ego-comp subtracts a linear guess of its own motion, which is wrong out at the edges — one more place alpha-beta’s feedback loop drifts. Watch the top-down cone swing to point at the target as the controller centres it. Controllers are the same math as bench/aim_sim.py.
